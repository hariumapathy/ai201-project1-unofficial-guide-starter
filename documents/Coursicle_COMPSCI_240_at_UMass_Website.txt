Description
Development of mathematical reasoning skills for problems that involve uncertainty. Counting and probability -- basic counting problems, probability definitions, mean, variance, binomial distribution, discrete random variables, continuous random variables, Markov and Chebyshev bounds, Laws of large numbers, and central limit theorem. Probabilistic reasoning -- conditional probability and odds, Bayes' Law, Markov Chains, Bayesian Networks. Enrollment Requirements: Open to COMPSCI or CMPLING majors. Prerequisites: a grade of C or better in CICS 160 (previously INFO 190T), COMPSCI 187, or CICS 210 AND a grade of C or better in MATH 132.
